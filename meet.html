Meet Amy

Building things that last.

A Charleston-based real estate professional, investor and entrepreneur with a passion for spaces that work as beautifully as they live.

Amy’s story

Experience from more than one side of the property.

For more than 15 years, Amy has renovated, staged and managed homes—from personal residences to high-performing rentals.

Her background blends hands-on experience in design and construction with knowledge of the Charleston market, investment strategy and long-term financial planning.

Amy believes real estate is most powerful when it is personal. Whether helping a family find the right home, managing a renovation or building a business, she approaches the work with care, clarity and attention to detail.
