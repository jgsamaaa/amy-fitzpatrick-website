Carolina North Side

An ocean-view side of the duplex with a generous deck, three bedrooms and a comfortable setup for up to eight guests.

The stay

Room to settle into the coast.

8 guests3 bedrooms4 beds2 bathrooms

The current site describes an updated side of the duplex with a new kitchen, refreshed main areas, a gated deck, ceiling fans and smooth ceilings. The sleeping setup includes a king bedroom, a queen bedroom and a queen-over-queen bunk room, plus a pack-and-play and a convertible twin chair/bed.

The space

The North Side is described as having ocean views, a large deck and ample parking. Linens, towels, beach chairs and eight beach towels are provided. The home is within walking distance of movies, groceries, miniature golf, a water park and go-karts.

Guest access

Guests have access to half of the duplex and half of the driveway. The current site states that the entire six-bedroom home can also be rented through a separate listing.

Important notes

The current source permits dogs only, with a maximum of two, and says guests should disclose pets when requesting a booking. It also notes an exterior camera in the carport, smart TVs without cable, septic-system care and North Carolina restrictions on grilling on decks and balconies. All rules must be verified on Airbnb before booking.
