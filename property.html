A home shaped around the view.

The current listing describes one of the larger residences in Governors Pointe, with broad marsh and Ashley River views, expansive living spaces and a flexible three-to-four-bedroom layout. The home includes four full bathrooms and was remodeled and expanded with long-term living in mind.

The heart of the home

A glass-wrapped solarium supported by cedar beams opens toward the Ipe deck and marsh. It is presented as a year-round gathering place for morning light and evenings by the water.

Neighborhood water access

The current listing states that the HOA includes access to a neighborhood dock, pavilion and kayak launch. It also describes an optional Boat Slip Association membership with a deeded slip for a boat up to 25 feet; these terms should be confirmed with the listing agent.

Connected to Charleston

The source listing describes the home as less than ten minutes from historic Charleston, Mount Pleasant or the airport, and approximately twenty minutes from Sullivan’s Island or Folly Beach. Travel times are approximate and should be independently verified.
