# Amy FitzPatrick Real Estate — redesign study and full-site plan

## Creative direction: Lowcountry Ledger

**Five-second feeling:** calm Charleston confidence — warm, personal, strategic, and established.

The redesign positions Amy as more than a conventional agent: a Charleston real-estate advisor with firsthand experience across buying, selling, investing, renovating, staging, and short-term rentals. The visual system uses authentic Lowcountry photography, marsh ink, oyster paper, restrained clay accents, high-contrast editorial serif typography, and a clean functional sans serif.

## What is worth preserving

- Amy’s personable, credible presence and strong portrait photography.
- Charleston and Lowcountry context.
- The unusually useful breadth of the business: buyer representation, seller representation, renovation/investment work, listings, and hosted stays.
- Authentic project photography, especially River Reach Way, Old Towne Road, James Island, and the Emerald Isle stays.
- Existing client testimonials and direct consultation capability.
- Verified contact email: `Amy@AmyFitzPatrick.com`.

## Main problems on the current site

1. **The homepage is too thin.** It introduces Amy and a testimonial but does not explain her full value, show properties or project work, or guide buyers, sellers, investors, and guests into clear journeys.
2. **The Property Listings page is broken.** The embedded app currently displays “This is not a valid link,” leaving a nearly empty page.
3. **Services are hidden from navigation.** The booking page exists but is not represented as a clear top-level buyer/seller path.
4. **The Airbnb page is a very long wall of thumbnails and unedited listing text.** “GET IT” is not meaningful CTA language, and the four options are difficult to compare.
5. **Portfolio presentation is visually sparse.** Strong photography exists, but the index offers little project context, categorization, or case-study value.
6. **Project detail pages are difficult to scan.** River Reach Way, for example, opens with one extremely long paragraph, weak facts hierarchy, no clear inquiry action, and excessive blank vertical space after the gallery.
7. **Navigation includes low-value commerce/account UI.** Cart and login controls add clutter without supporting the primary real-estate journey.
8. **Copy and SEO are inconsistent.** The homepage browser title focuses on “How to Invest in Real Estate with No Money,” while the visible experience positions Amy as a broad Charleston agent.
9. **There is a visible typo:** “BOOK A CONSULATION.”
10. **Contact data needs reconciliation.** The visible footer shows `843-813-9505`, while the homepage telephone link resolved to `+1-843-580-1743` during inspection. Confirm the correct public number before launch.

1. **Buy + Sell**
2. **Properties**

Cart and login are removed unless the client identifies a real customer-account requirement.

## Page-by-page redesign system

### 1. Home

Narrative: Charleston promise → service paths → featured property → Amy’s point of view → selected work → stays → testimonial → consultation.

Primary actions:

- Book a consultation
- Explore properties

### 2. Buy + Sell

- Buyer and seller choice immediately above the fold.
- Clear process timelines instead of generic service cards.
- Consultation options with duration, current fee, inclusions, and cancellation policy.
- Relocation guidance and first-time-buyer reassurance.
- Seller preparation, positioning, marketing, and closing process.
- Testimonials relevant to each journey.
- One booking action after the visitor understands the value.

### 3. Properties

- Replace or repair the invalid Real Estate App / IDX connection.
- Search and filter by location, price, beds, baths, status, and property type.
- Clean list/map switch on desktop; list-first mobile flow.
- Amy-curated featured properties above the full inventory when appropriate.
- Honest empty and unavailable states; never leave an embed error visible.

### 4. Property detail template

- Full-width photography-led arrival.
- Address/status and a compact fact rail.
- Price only from the verified feed or agent-approved listing data.
- Short editorial summary, then scannable chapters: Home, Setting, Details, Location.
- Gallery with intentional crops rather than a tiny thumbnail grid.
- Sticky desktop / accessible mobile inquiry CTA.
- Similar properties only after the primary listing story and inquiry path.

### 5. Portfolio

- Filters: All / Listings / Renovations / Investment + Stays.
- Unequal editorial image grid rather than repetitive equal cards.
- Each project includes role, objective, approach, and outcome when the client can verify them.
- Project-detail template built as a case study, not a raw text dump.

### 6. Stays

- Four clearly differentiated stay entries:
  - Carolina House, Emerald Isle
  - Carolina North Side
  - Carolina South Side
  - Charleston House
- Comparison essentials: guests, bedrooms, beds, baths, location, and verified booking destination.
- Edited guest-facing summaries; full rules and policies remain on the booking destination or a dedicated detail page.
- Use “View stay” / “Check availability,” not “GET IT.”
- No fabricated availability, ratings, or nightly rates.

### 7. Meet Amy

- Portrait-led editorial introduction.
- 15+ years of renovation, staging, and property-management experience.
- Personal approach and Charleston market perspective.
- Community/professional roles only after client revalidation.
- Social links and consultation close.

### 8. Contact / consultation

- Buyer, seller, investor/renovation, stay, and general-inquiry intent options.
- Name, email, phone, preferred contact method, timeline, and message.
- Clear expectation for response time once Amy confirms it.
- Booking integration for the existing consultation products.
