Carolina South Side

The oceanside half of the duplex with an updated kitchen, three bedrooms and beach access only steps away.

The stay

Room to settle into the coast.

8 guests3 bedrooms4 beds2 bathrooms

The current site describes a three-bedroom, two-bath oceanside duplex for up to eight guests. Its fully stocked kitchen opens from the living area, while a separate dining area seats eight. The sleeping setup includes a king bedroom, a queen bedroom and a room with a bunk bed and queen bed.

The space

Updates listed on the current site include a new kitchen, new gated deck, refreshed ceilings and drywall in the main areas, paint, ceiling fans and new lighting. The listing also mentions monthly discounts by request.

Guest access

Guests have access to the full South Side, half of the driveway and the shower and grill area under the deck.

Important notes

The source page allows dogs only, with a maximum of two, and notes an exterior camera in the carport. It also describes linens and made beds, smart TVs without cable, septic-system care and restrictions on grilling on decks or balconies. All policies must be confirmed on the final Airbnb listing.
